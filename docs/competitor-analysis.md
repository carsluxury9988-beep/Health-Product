## Research method and confidence

Pages were fetched directly on 28 September 2026. Alpro's homepage copy was readable; Guardian's page was mostly client-side JavaScript; BIG Pharmacy returned a store-availability prompt; Shopee exposed its localized search shell but not individual products; Watsons returned 403; Lazada returned a security challenge. Product-specific pages, prices, promotions, shipping terms, COD availability, reviews, returns and delivery times therefore remain **unverified** for these sources.

The review does not confirm that any competitor sells Magnum Pump, Ultrahot or Horsemen. The only product-name marketplace query attempted was Hammer of Thor, and the listing results were not available to inspect. No competitor prices or claims are repeated as facts.

## Site opportunities

- Make the supplied RM159 price and free-delivery offer easy to find, with no fabricated reference price or discount.
- Explain that an order form sends a request, then the seller confirms availability and destination-specific COD before dispatch.
- Use a calm, mobile-first catalogue with short routes to product facts, FAQs, contact and order.
- Show missing label data as missing; do not borrow competitor descriptions, certification claims, testimonials, imagery or urgency tactics.
- Re-run this benchmark manually from Malaysia before launch, including product pages, checkout, COD restrictions and return policies. Save dated links/screenshots for any claims used in future comparison.
